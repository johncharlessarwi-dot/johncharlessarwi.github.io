What to expect when working with JOHN ONLINE SERVICES AND APPLICATIONS — from choosing the right solution to getting started.

What is JOHN ONLINE SERVICES AND APPLICATIONS?

JOHN ONLINE SERVICES AND APPLICATIONS provides digital services including custom websites and web applications, business management systems, POS and ERP solutions, inventory tools, and AI-enabled workflows. The aim is to turn practical business or organisational needs into useful software.

Who are these services for?

The services are intended for individuals, startups, small and medium-sized businesses, and organisations that need a website, a tailored digital system, or help improving an existing workflow.

How much does a website or custom system cost?

There is no single fixed price for every project. Cost depends on the required features, complexity, integrations, delivery scope, and ongoing support. Share your requirements and estimated budget through the project form to request a tailored quotation before committing.

Can you integrate existing tools, APIs, databases, or payment services?

Integration requirements can be discussed during project discovery. Compatibility depends on the third-party provider, available API or documentation, access permissions, and technical requirements; the exact integration scope is confirmed before work begins.

Should I choose custom software or an off-the-shelf alternative?

An existing product or spreadsheet may be the better choice when it already handles your process well. Custom software is worth considering when your workflow has specific requirements, several processes need to work together, or standard tools create limitations. We can discuss both approaches before you decide.

How does onboarding and project delivery work?

Start by sending a project request with your goals and required features. We then discuss the requirements, confirm the proposed scope, estimate and milestones, and agree on the next steps. Once approved, the solution is developed, reviewed and tested with you before launch and handover.
