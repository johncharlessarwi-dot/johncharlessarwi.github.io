Business decisions
carry uncertainty.

Many business tools report historical activity. BizFlow AI explores a complementary question: how might a proposed decision affect the business? Owners need a way to compare options without confusing assumptions, estimates and actual financial records.

THE PROBLEM

Decisions made without a clear comparison

Price changes, stock purchases, marketing spend, staffing and expansion can affect revenue, expenses, cash position and margins. Looking at one metric alone can hide important trade-offs.

THE PRODUCT DIRECTION

Make assumptions visible

Structure a business question into a scenario, compare a projected state with an available baseline, explain uncertainty and leave the final decision with the business owner.

From question
to insight.

The intended decision workflow keeps calculations, source data and AI explanations distinct. Missing business information should be requested or disclosed rather than silently invented.

01AskDescribe a business decision in natural language.
02GroundIdentify available records, inputs and assumptions.
03SimulateCalculate supported scenario outcomes.
04CompareReview trade-offs, risks and uncertainty.
Product principle: actual records, historical observations, user-provided assumptions, estimates and projections must remain clearly distinguishable. A projection is not a guarantee.
