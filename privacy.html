Privacy Policy.

This page explains what information may be collected when you browse the website or contact JOHN ONLINE SERVICES AND APPLICATIONS, and how it is used.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Project enquiries

If you submit the project form, it asks for your name, email address, optional phone/WhatsApp number and company, requested service, optional budget and timeline, preferred contact method, and project description. The form sends these details to the website's contact endpoint so the enquiry can be received and answered. You can also contact the business directly by email or WhatsApp.

Responding and delivering services

Information is used to respond to enquiries, clarify requirements, prepare project estimates, communicate about work, provide requested services, maintain website security and prevent spam or misuse. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the general project form.

Technical and interaction data

The website may process basic technical information through its hosting platform. Its analytics script queues selected interaction events such as WhatsApp, email and phone link clicks and project-form submissions in the browser dataLayer. That script does not itself configure an analytics vendor, measurement ID, cookie or external analytics request. Other platform-level logging or tools, if enabled by the hosting provider, are governed by that provider's practices.

External providers and links

The website uses or links to services such as website hosting, Google Fonts, GitHub's public API, GitHub-hosted profile images, WhatsApp and email providers. When your browser connects to those services or you follow an external link, the relevant provider may receive technical or account information under its own privacy policy. We do not control third-party services.

Limited access

Enquiry information may be processed by the technical providers needed to host the website, operate the contact endpoint and deliver communications. It is not published as portfolio content. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

How long information is kept

Enquiry information should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a project, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes or meet applicable obligations. Actual retention may also depend on the systems and service providers used to process the enquiry.

Access or correction

You may contact the business to ask about personal information you have submitted, request correction of inaccurate details, or ask for deletion where appropriate. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate project, security or record-keeping reasons.

Questions about privacy

This policy may change as website features or data practices change. The current revision date is shown above. For privacy questions or requests, email johncharlessarwi@gmail.com or contact WhatsApp +255 698 170 989.
